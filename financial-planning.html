Roth IRAs

Think of a Roth IRA as a way to pay taxes on the seed, not the harvest. By contributing after-tax dollars now, you can enjoy decades of potential tax-free growth and withdrawals later, helping you keep more of your retirement savings.

We help you decide whether a Roth fits your situation, how it works alongside a 401(k) or traditional IRA, and how much to contribute each year.

Financial planning FAQ

Questions about planning with us.

Don't see yours? Call (952) 432-8000.

What is holistic financial planning?

It means looking at your whole financial life at once: investments, retirement, taxes, insurance, college savings, your business, and your estate. Instead of managing one account in isolation, we coordinate every decision so they work together.

Do you only manage investments?

No. We're holistic planners, not just asset managers. Investment management is one part of a plan that also covers retirement income, taxes, saving for kids, and estate considerations. If a need falls outside what we do, we connect you with trusted professionals.

Should I roll over my old 401(k)?

It depends on your situation. A rollover IRA may offer more flexibility and investment choices, but staying in a former employer's plan can make sense too. We walk through the options with you before anything moves.

Do you work with clients outside Burnsville and Barron?

Yes. We meet in person at our Burnsville, MN and Barron, WI offices, and by video and phone wherever you live, including the Milwaukee area.
