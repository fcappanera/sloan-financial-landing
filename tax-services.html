Tax services FAQ

Questions about taxes and bookkeeping.

Don't see yours? Call (952) 432-8000.

Do you prepare taxes for small businesses?

Yes. We prepare returns for individuals and businesses, and for business owners we coordinate the business and personal side so decisions work together.

What is the difference between tax preparation and tax planning?

Tax preparation looks back: it files last year's return accurately. Tax planning looks ahead: it helps you make decisions during the year that can lower future taxes. We do both.

Do you still do financial planning during tax season?

Yes. We never put financial planning on hold for tax season. You can call us about your plan any month of the year.

What is the difference between Bookkeeping and Bookkeeping Plus?

Bookkeeping keeps your records accurate and organized. Bookkeeping Plus adds financial reporting, cash flow visibility, and strategic advice, like an outsourced CFO.

Can I work with you remotely?

Yes. Many clients work with us by video, phone, and secure document exchange. You can also meet us in person in Burnsville, MN or Barron, WI.
