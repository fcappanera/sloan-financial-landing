Why Sloan exists

"There is infinitely more to your financial plan than how your IRA is performing."

I kept seeing the same thing. People would come in with no plan, no advisor, or an advisor who only discussed the account he managed for them. Nobody was looking at the whole picture: the retirement question, the cabin they've been thinking about, the loans, the taxes, the business.

I've always been good with numbers, problem solving, and teaching. My mom was a teacher, and I tutored kids in math from high school all the way through college. Financial advising was the natural fit: solve the problem with the numbers, then teach people how to manage their money more efficiently.

Our goal is simple. We want you to sleep easy at night knowing your whole financial plan is taken care of, not just that your 401(k) is invested appropriately.

David Sloan Founder, Sloan Financial Solutions

About David

David founded Sloan Financial Solutions at the beginning of 2020. Before that, he spent several years as a registered assistant at another financial advising practice, and before that he worked on Cargill's risk management floor.

Day to day, David meets with clients to understand where they stand and what their next steps should be. Outside of meetings, he and the team manage client investment accounts, review performance, and evaluate new opportunities for clients.

His favorite part of the job is lifting the weight off a question someone has been dreading for years. "Seeing the weight of some of these questions being lifted off of people brings me a lot of fulfillment."

David graduated from UW–La Crosse in 2015 with a double major in Statistics, with an actuarial emphasis, and Finance. He grew up in Barron, Wisconsin, and now lives in Burnsville with his wife, Amanda, their sons Knox, Porter, and Ollie, and Brinkley, their Wheaten Terrier. When he's not in the office, he's fishing, hunting, landscaping, walking in the woods, or coaching youth sports.

Tax services

About Adam

Adam leads the tax side of Sloan Financial Solutions. Before joining, he spent a little over ten years with Associated Bank, primarily in risk management, and started his career doing actuarial work in the retirement space at Willis Towers Watson.

Adam and David met in college and quickly became friends, taking many of the same courses. When David set out to add tax services, bringing in one of his closest friends was a natural fit.

His work goes beyond compiling data and submitting a return. Adam focuses on accurate, timely preparation and on the strategic tax moves that strengthen a client's overall plan. "Even if we identify just one opportunity that someone didn't know was available to them, that can make a meaningful difference over time."

Adam holds a bachelor's degree in Mathematics and Statistics with an Actuarial Science emphasis and a minor in Business from UW–La Crosse. He grew up in Milwaukee and lives in Big Bend, Wisconsin, with his wife, Sadie, their boys Finn and Mac, and two Springer Spaniels, Cocoa and Juan Pablo. "Needless to say, our house stays pretty busy." He still plays baseball and softball when he can, often with his dad, and golf is "definitely an obsession."
