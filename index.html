Burnsville, MN FAQ

Questions people ask before they call.

Don't see yours? Call (952) 432-8000.

Why work with a local financial advisor in Burnsville?

You get to sit down face to face with the person managing your plan, in an office close to home. We work with families and business owners across Burnsville, Apple Valley, Eagan, Lakeville, Savage, and Prior Lake, and meet by video when that is easier.

What does a first meeting look like?

It is a conversation, not a sales pitch. We talk about your goals, what is worrying you, and the decisions in front of you. If it makes sense to keep going, we outline what a plan would include and what it would cost.

How are you paid?

We explain exactly how we are compensated before you commit to anything. Depending on the service, that may be an advisory fee, a planning fee, or commissions on certain products. Details are in our Form CRS.

Can you help with my business retirement plan?

Yes. We help business owners set up and manage 401(k) plans, small business retirement plans, profit-sharing plans, and deferred compensation, and we plan for succession or the sale of the business.

Do you help with college savings?

Yes. We help families choose and fund 529 plans and UTMA accounts, and fit college savings into the rest of the plan so it does not come at the expense of retirement.

Do I need a minimum amount to work with you?

We work with clients of all ages and walks of life. Call us and we will tell you honestly whether we are a good fit.
