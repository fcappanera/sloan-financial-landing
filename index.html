Meet David Sloan

"There is infinitely more to your financial plan than how your IRA is performing."

David started Sloan Financial Solutions in 2020 after seeing too many people with no plan, no advisor, or an advisor who only talked about the one account he managed. His favorite part of the job is helping someone finally answer the question they've been dreading for years: "Can I retire?" "Can I buy a cabin?" "How do I pay off my loans?"

He grew up in Barron, Wisconsin, and lives in Burnsville with his wife, Amanda, their three sons, and Brinkley, the family's Wheaten Terrier.

Why work with a local financial advisor in Burnsville?

You get to sit down face to face with the person managing your plan, in an office close to home. We work with families and business owners across Burnsville, Apple Valley, Eagan, Savage, Prior Lake, Lakeville, and Shakopee, and meet by video when that is easier.

Do you have a financial advisor office in Barron, WI?

Yes. Our Barron office is at 421 E La Salle Ave, Suite A, run by Eric Holten, a Barron native. David grew up in Barron too, so for us it's home. We serve Rice Lake, Cameron, Chetek, Spooner, Cumberland, and Hayward from there. Call (715) 418-2044 to meet in person.

Do you work with clients outside Burnsville and Barron?

Yes. We work with clients wherever they live, meeting by video and phone. That includes the Milwaukee area, where Adam SanFelippo, our tax and accounting advisor, is based.

What does a first meeting look like?

It is a conversation, not a sales pitch. We talk about your goals, what is worrying you, and the decisions in front of you. If it makes sense to keep going, we outline what a plan would include and what it would cost.

How are you paid?

We explain exactly how we are compensated before you commit to anything. Depending on the service, that may be an advisory fee, a planning fee, or commissions on certain products. Details are in our Form CRS.

Can you help with my business retirement plan?

Yes. We help business owners set up and manage 401(k) plans, small business retirement plans, profit-sharing plans, and deferred compensation, and we plan for succession or the sale of the business.

Do you offer bookkeeping for small businesses?

Yes. We handle income, expenses, invoices, payroll, and cash flow tracking, and our Bookkeeping Plus service adds reporting and strategic advice, like an outsourced CFO at a fraction of the cost.

Do you help with college savings?

Yes. We help families choose and fund 529 plans and UTMA accounts, and fit college savings into the rest of the plan so it does not come at the expense of retirement.

Do I need a minimum amount to work with you?

We work with clients of all ages and walks of life. Call us and we will tell you honestly whether we are a good fit.
