Common questions

Things people ask before they call.

Don't see yours? Call (952) 432-8000. You'll get a straight answer.

What does a fiduciary financial advisor do?

A fiduciary financial advisor is required to act in your best interest. At Sloan Financial Solutions that means recommendations are built around your goals, your timeline, and your risk tolerance, not around products.

Who do you work with?

We work with families, professionals, pre-retirees, and business owners in Burnsville, the south metro, and across the Twin Cities. Many clients come to us at a transition point: a new business, a growing family, a sale, or retirement.

Do you offer tax preparation and tax planning?

Yes. We prepare individual and small business tax returns and coordinate tax planning with your overall financial plan, so decisions about retirement contributions, business income, and investments are made with taxes in mind.

How do you charge for your services?

We explain fees clearly before you commit to anything. Depending on the service, compensation may be a planning fee, an asset-based advisory fee, or a flat fee for tax preparation. Full details are available in our Form CRS.

What happens in a first meeting?

The first conversation is about you, not paperwork. We talk through your goals, your concerns, and the decisions in front of you. From there we outline what a plan could look like and you decide whether it makes sense to move forward.

Is there a minimum to work with you?

We do not believe good advice should wait until you have a certain net worth. Reach out and we will be honest about whether we are the right fit.
